Process

How a project runs.

Every project follows the same five steps, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Brief

    You tell me about your business, your buyer and the goal of the page.

  2. Research

    I study your audience, competitors and the searches your buyers make.

  3. Write

    I draft copy that answers what the reader needs to understand, trust and do.

  4. Optimize

    Keywords, meta titles and structure are worked in so the page can rank.

  5. Revise & deliver

    We refine it together, and you get clean, ready-to-publish copy.
